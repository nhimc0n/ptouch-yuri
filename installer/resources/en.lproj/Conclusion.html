Installation complete

  1. Open the PT-E850TKW app from Launchpad or the Applications folder.
  2. Check that the status card says Ready to print. If there is no printer address yet, enter the IP address or the printer's name (like BRW….local) under "Printer address".
  3. Choose the cut, quality and label size and press Apply. From then on, print from any app with the PT-E850TKW printer.

Install log: /var/log/ptouch-e850-install.log
