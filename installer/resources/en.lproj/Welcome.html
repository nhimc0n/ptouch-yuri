Brother PT-E850TKW label printer

This installer adds the PT-E850TKW printer to macOS so you can print labels from your browser and any app, and the PT-E850TKW app to choose the cut, the quality and the label size.

  • Installs the driver and creates the printer. The installer looks for the printer on your network.
  • Installs the settings app in the Applications folder.

The printer connects over the network (Wi-Fi or cable). Turn it on and connect it to the same network as this computer before you install.
